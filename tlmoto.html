Overview

What I did

As a member of the Structures team I contributed to the mechanical and structural development of an electric racing motorcycle. My work covered CAD, structural analysis, optimisation, composite design and manufacturing, with a focus on lightweight, structurally efficient components.

Mechanical design

Structural and mechanical components in 3D CAD, considering requirements, interfaces, packaging and manufacturability.

Structural analysis

FEM of motorcycle components under different loading conditions to evaluate performance and drive iterations.

Optimisation

Topology optimisation to investigate material reduction while keeping the required structural behaviour.

Composites & manufacturing

Carbon-fibre lay-up, resin application, mould preparation, demoulding, finishing and CNC-machined moulds.

01 / 04

Carbon-fibre swingarm

One of my main projects. I initiated the exploration of a carbon-fibre alternative to the motorcycle's existing metal swingarm, focusing on the early redesign stages.

I developed and evaluated initial concepts through structural simulation, using different CAD configurations to investigate structural behaviour and the feasibility of the proposal.

02 / 04

Triple-clamp topology optimisation

I worked on the topology optimisation of a triple clamp to reduce material while maintaining structural performance, combining optimisation, CAD development and structural analysis over several design iterations.

Initial design

Topology optimisation

The optimisation identified regions where material could be removed under the defined constraints.

Design development

The results served as a reference for refining the geometry through successive CAD iterations.

Structural analysis

Analyses on successive iterations evaluated behaviour under the defined loads.

03 / 04

Motorcycle frame design

I contributed to the frame from initial concept selection through CAD development and structural analysis, weighing structural performance, weight, manufacturing approach, cost and integration with the motorcycle.

Concept development

Several concepts were compared, including different trellis-frame configurations and alternatives in composite and metallic structures, judged on expected structural behaviour, efficiency, manufacturability and overall suitability.

Selected architecture

Vehicle geometry & packaging

Structural analysis

The finite-element model defined the mesh, boundary conditions, loads, reference points and structural connections.

Design iteration & dynamic analysis

An intermediate version was analysed under structural loading; a later analysis added the suspension and spring-damper behaviour to represent a more realistic dynamic load case.

Final frame

The final frame has local material-reduction features that cut weight while keeping the required structural performance, linking concept selection, CAD, analysis and lightweight design.

04 / 04

Composite manufacturing

Alongside CAD and analysis, I gained hands-on experience manufacturing carbon-fibre motorcycle components, particularly fairings: MDF mould preparation, CNC machining, surface preparation, lay-up, resin application, demoulding and finishing. It gave me practical insight into how design and manufacturability relate.
